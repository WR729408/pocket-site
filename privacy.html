Pocket privacy policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Pocket is a private, single-user tool that runs on its owner's own computers. This page explains what it does with data from the owner's Google account.

What Pocket reads from Google

  • Gmail (read-only): recent inbox messages (sender, subject, date and text), to find what needs an answer.
  • Google Calendar: events, to start recording a scheduled call, to name recordings, and to check the owner's availability when preparing a reply.
  • Google Drive (file names only): to point to the right document in a draft.
  • Google Tasks and Google Sheets: to keep the owner's task list and tracker sheet up to date.

What Pocket writes to Google

Pocket sends an email or creates a calendar event only after the owner has seen the full message or event and tapped "Accept & send". It also updates the owner's own task list and tracker sheet. It never sends anything on its own.

Where the data goes

  • Google data is processed on the owner's own computers, by models that run locally.
  • When the owner chooses, a message Pocket flagged may be passed to Anthropic's Claude, under the owner's own Claude account, to prepare a draft.
  • Google data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else. People do not read it except the owner.
  • Google data is never used to train or improve AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Pocket's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting data

Pocket keeps what it needs in a database on the owner's computer. The owner can delete it at any time. Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
