Pocket

A personal meeting recorder and assistant.

Pocket records conversations and calls, writes notes and action items from them, and keeps a plan of what needs doing. With permission, it reads its owner's email and calendar to notice what needs an answer, and prepares drafts for the owner to review on their phone.

Pocket is a private, single-user tool. It runs on its owner's own computers and uses local models. Nothing is sent from the owner's Google account unless the owner taps "Accept & send" on the exact message or event first.
